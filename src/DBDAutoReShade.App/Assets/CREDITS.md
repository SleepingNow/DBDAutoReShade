# Credits

DBD Auto ReShade is an unofficial fan tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Behaviour Interactive.

The map pictures, the fonts and the sound below are embedded in DBDAutoReShade.exe. This file and the font licence texts ship beside the exe in its Licenses folder.

## Map pictures

The map pictures are Behaviour Interactive's in-game map icons from Dead by Daylight, obtained via the Dead by Daylight Wiki (https://deadbydaylight.wiki.gg). Dead by Daylight and its artwork are trademarks and copyright of Behaviour Interactive Inc.

The pictures are the originals: cropped to the picture (the ragged torn frame removed) and saved losslessly. They are not upscaled or otherwise altered by any upscaler or AI tool.

## Sound

The "Great" skill-check sound is an original synthesized recreation made for this app (variant "Shing"). It contains no audio from Dead by Daylight. It is stored as 16-bit PCM, 44.1 kHz, mono, normalized to a -1.0 dBFS peak; the app plays it at volume² of that level (50 % ≈ -13 dBFS).
